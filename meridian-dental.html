About Meridian

Led by dentists who explain before they treat.

Dr. Amara Osei founded Meridian in 2010 on one rule: no procedure without a plain explanation first. That rule still shapes every visit today, from a first cleaning to full smile makeovers.

  • Board-certified team, six specialties on site
  • Digital X-rays with 80% less radiation
  • Open evenings and Saturdays
