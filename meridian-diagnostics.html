About Meridian

Calibrated equipment. Qualified eyes on every result.

Meridian Diagnostics is a NABL-accredited centre built around one idea: a report is only useful if it's accurate and easy to read. Every sample is processed on calibrated instruments and reviewed by our in-house pathologists before it reaches you.

Our team includes certified lab technicians, radiologists, and a cardiologist on call — so results come with context, not just numbers.
